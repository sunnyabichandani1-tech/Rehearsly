Your Gemini key

Everything smart in Rehearsly runs on one free key from Google: the interviewer, the scoring, and the voice. Get yours free at aistudio.google.com/apikey and paste it below. It is saved only in this browser and sent only to Google. It is never written into the app's code, so it stays private even though the app is public.

Live voice interview

When this is on, the AI Interviewer opens a real time voice line, like a phone call. It hears you while you speak, works out by itself when you have finished, replies in a real voice, and you can cut it off mid sentence. It runs on the same free Gemini key above. If it cannot connect, the interviewer quietly falls back to typing. Turn it off here if you would rather always type. The model name below is a preview model, so if Google renames it one day, paste the new name here.

If the interviewer cuts in while you are still thinking, pick a longer pause here.

This one voice is used everywhere in the app: the interviewer, the Listen button, the story bank and the practice questions, always with the accent above. If it sounds too soft, change the manner above, and try the deeper voices. Charon, Fenrir, Orus, Alnilam, Rasalgethi and Enceladus are the weightier ones. Tap Hear this voice to listen before you commit. Google does not let an accent be set directly, so the accent comes from how the interviewer is briefed, and different voices carry it differently.

Where your interviews are saved

Your scores, feedback and the full transcript of every interview are saved in this browser, on this device only. They are not sent to your Google Sheet and they are not on any server. That means they are private to you, but it also means clearing this browser's data, or switching to another device, loses them. Download a copy here to keep them safe.

Backup voice

Only used for a moment if the Gemini voice cannot be reached. Pick the one that sounds closest to your main voice, so the switch is less jarring.

Sections

Every section can be renamed, given a new icon, or hidden, using the pencil next to it in the menu. The three that ship with the app, the roadmaps, the story bank and the action plan, are only hidden rather than deleted, so you can always bring them back here.
